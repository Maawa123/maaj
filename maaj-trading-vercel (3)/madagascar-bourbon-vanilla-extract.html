Madagascar Bourbon Vanilla Extract Alcohol-Free

← Back

A concentrated extract obtained through water extraction, with no trace of alcohol whatsoever designed for productions with religious or dietary requirements, without compromising on aromatic intensity.

  • Technical name

    AF20X Oleoresin

  • Origin

    Madagascar

  • Extraction method

    Distilled water extraction + vacuum concentration

  • Alcohol

    None

  • Vanillin content

    2.1-2.4%

  • Appearance

    Thick dark brown liquid

  • Shelf life

    Up to 18 months, stored cool (~4°C), away from light

Stage II · In the workshop

From pod to extract

A process designed to preserve the aromatic integrity of the vanilla, without ever resorting to alcohol or synthetic solvents.

  1. 01

    Soaking

    The dried, conditioned pods are cut and left to soak: their aromatic compounds begin to release.

  2. 02

    Water extraction

    Where conventional methods use alcohol, our partner extracts the aromas with distilled water only.

  3. 03

    Vacuum concentration

    At low temperature, to protect the most fragile molecules. This stage sets the concentration: 10X, 20X.

  4. 04

    Packing and control

    Stored away from light and heat, with specific gravity and vanillin content checked batch after batch.

Full control of the process is what sets a producer-processor apart from a trader.

Our Quality Commitment

This alcohol-free extract is made solely from dried vanilla pods and water — no alcoholic solvent is used in its production. Ideal for pastry chefs and chocolatiers with customers sensitive to this requirement, it retains the full aromatic richness of Bourbon Madagascar vanilla. A Halal declaration from our producer partner is available on request for this product.
