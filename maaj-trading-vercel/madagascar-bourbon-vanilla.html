Stage I · On the plantation

From vine to harvest

Nine months of growing, then several weeks of curing.

  1. 01

    Pollination and growing

    Every flower is pollinated by hand, one by one. Grown without pesticides or chemical fertiliser, the pods develop over about nine months.

  2. 02

    The harvest

    Too early, the pod has not developed its potential; too late, it loses quality. Picking happens just as the tip begins to turn yellow.

  3. 03

    Curing

    Scalding, sweating, then gradual drying, under strict control of moisture content, brought down to 25-28% where needed.

  4. 04

    Conditioning and grading

    Sorted and graded by hand (16 cm and up for the Gourmet), then packed in paraffined paper so the pod keeps breathing.

You never rush a vanilla. You wait until it is ready.

Our Quality Commitment

Our Madagascar Bourbon Gourmet pod is hand-selected and carefully graded black, supple and glossy, both generous and intense. Every flower is hand-pollinated, and the pods spend nine months growing before several weeks of traditional curing (scalding, sweating, gradual drying). Sourced directly from our producer-processor partner, with full control at every stage, it suits infusions and creams as well as preparations where the whole pod needs to remain visible.
